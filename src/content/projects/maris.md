## System Overview

The **Marine Activity Reporting Information System (MARIS)** is a specialized portal built to standardize, log, and analyze field activity reports across maritime operations.

### Key Capabilities & Architecture
- **Activity Logging Pipeline**: Digitizes manual field logs into structured data entries for rapid searchability and reporting.
- **Field Telemetry & Monitoring**: Tracks operational status and marine reporting logs in real time.
- **Reporting Analytics**: Generates consolidated summary reports for regional supervisors and field monitors.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Enforces access permissions across operational staff, field reporters, and administrative auditors.
