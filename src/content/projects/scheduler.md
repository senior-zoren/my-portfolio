## System Overview

**Scheduler** is an executive calendar and resource allocation system designed to manage executive availability, prevent meeting conflicts, and formalize appointment requests across DICT offices.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Executive Calendar Visibility**: Displays synchronized availability grids while maintaining privacy controls over confidential meeting details.
- **Multi-Tier Request Pipeline**: Allows staff to request appointments that can be vetted and pre-approved by executive personnel prior to final confirmation.
- **Conflict Management Engine**: Real-time slot locking preventing overlapping booking requests for shared executive schedules and meeting rooms.
- **Automated Notifications**: Dispatches confirmation alerts and status updates to requesters upon approval or rescheduling.
