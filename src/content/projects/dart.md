## System Overview

**DART (Accomplishment Report Tracker)** is an internal data banking and reporting platform built for **DICT Region VIII**. Designed with portability, transparency, and rapid data retrieval in mind, DART serves as a single source of truth for regional accomplishment metrics frequently requested by DICT Central Office Project Management Teams, members of Congress, Provincial Governors, and Local Chief Executives (Mayors).

By replacing fragmented manual reports with real-time digital submissions, DART provides executive visibility into project deliverables across key national ICT programs.

---

## Core Capabilities & Executive Architecture

### 1. Multi-Program Data Banking & Categorization
- **Program-Specific Ingestion**: Categorizes regional accomplishments across core DICT flagship programs, including **Cybersecurity, Free Wi-Fi for All, GovNet, DRRM, ILCDB, IIDB, PNPKI, NBP, NIPPSB, DGP,** and **MISS**.
- **Centralized Query Bank**: Functions as a digital repository allowing quick aggregation of field status data for legislative inquiries, executive briefings, and inter-agency requests.

### 2. Executive Analytics & Performance Leaderboard
- **Accomplishment Breakdown**: Real-time project-by-project breakdown displaying raw submission volumes and progress tracking.
- **Division Activity Meters & Leaderboards**: Tracks team engagement and submission distribution metrics across divisions to encourage accountability and timely reporting.

### 3. Role-Based Governance & Auditability
- **Control Panel & Logs**: Comprehensive administrative control panel featuring system activity logs and archived records for transparent auditability.
- **Regional Stakeholder Transparency**: Streamlines information delivery so regional leadership can present accurate, verified data to external government stakeholders on demand.
